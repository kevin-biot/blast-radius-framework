# Claims and Evidence Policy

**Version:** 0.1
**Reviewed:** 2026-10-26

## Scope

BRF describes reachable operational exposure under a stated deployment boundary, threat model and recovery horizon. Architecture constrains exposure; model behaviour, data, operators and configuration influence failure probability and realised severity.

| Conclusion | Required evidence |
|---|---|
| Architectural exposure | Deployed permissions, reachable services, interfaces, state and recovery tests |
| Evidence readiness | Provenance, test coverage, integrity verification and unresolved items |
| Legal applicability or compliance | Legal role, use category, applicable provisions/dates and substantive evidence |
| Insurance outcome | Insurer/product, insured entity/scope, geography, exclusions/limits, decision and date |

Without an inspected insurer decision or policy, record **coverage not established**. BR-4/BR-5, missing I1/I2 or an experimental score cannot establish universal uninsurability. Controls cannot establish that an exclusion does not apply. Distinguish insurers, MGAs and assurance schemes.

Public offerings include [AIUC's ElevenLabs announcement](https://aiuc.com/updates/elevenlabs-secures-first-of-its-kind-ai-agent-insurance), [HSB's AI liability announcement](https://www.munichre.com/hsb/en/press-and-publications/press-releases/2026/2026-03-18-introducing-ai-liability-insurance-for-small-businesses.html), and [Armilla's product description](https://www.armilla.ai/). These establish offerings, not coverage for a rated deployment.

## Quantification

The cardinal index and Kalman extension are experimental. Publish normalization, weights, target metric, exposure period, data, observation model, covariance assumptions and validation. Covariance of a compliance index is not automatically uncertainty in an insured-loss distribution. The earlier identity `upsilon = sigma_B` and direct premium implications are withdrawn as established results.

Composition equations are conditional models. Maximum single-event exposure, cumulative loss, bypass probability and voter error are different quantities. Unconditional probability products require independence; conditional products require the relevant conditional estimates. Repeated bounded actions can accumulate loss. Common causes invalidate naive independent-voter estimates.

The v0.5 worldview floor remains a conservative BRF policy pending [execution-boundary testing](./execution-boundary-research.md). Prose input does not itself establish unrestricted execution. A typed JSON envelope does not establish semantic bounds. Protocol names do not determine BR class.

## Evidence and Authority

Distinguish observation, repeatable test, independent reproduction, formal argument and market/legal determination. Empirical rates need apparatus, denominators, model/scaffold versions and uncertainty. A private source hash identifies a version but does not provide independent public reproduction.

Record organisational mandate, technical delegation and runtime enforcement separately. Runtime verification can establish credential bindings, scope, audience, expiry and observed enforcement under stated trust assumptions; it cannot establish the legal validity of a corporate mandate itself.

Signing and anchoring support integrity and attribution. They do not prove event truth, completeness or legal admissibility. Schema validation does not verify a signature or execute an invariant test.

## Historical Assessments

The April assessments retain their reported numeric profiles and experimental provenance. Their blanket conclusions about coverage, exclusions, lawful deployment and actuarial priceability are withdrawn as current conclusions. Use current crosswalks and actual insurer/legal determinations. This applies equally to Lane2's favourable coverage claims and the chatty-card pattern's unfavourable claims. Neither public assessment supplied an inspected policy or underwriting decision.
